Clinical Synthesis Notes

    Documented Uncertainties & Limitations

      🩻 Vision Model Card (DenseNet121-CXR)

      • Architecture: DenseNet-121 with feature concatenation
      • Modality: Frontal Chest Radiographs (PA / AP)
      • Resolution: 224 x 224 pixels, ImageNet normalized
      • Pathologies: Normal, Cardiomegaly, Pneumonia, Pleural Effusion, Consolidation, Atelectasis, Infiltration
      • Visual Localization: Real Grad-CAM on denseblock4 conv2 layer
      • Limitation: Experimental screening only; requires radiologist verification.

      📊 Risk Model Card (Calibrated Gradient Boosting)

      • Model: GradientBoostingClassifier + CalibratedClassifierCV
      • Dataset: Kaggle 918-Patient Heart Failure Dataset (fedesoriano)
      • Validation ROC-AUC: 0.9274
      • Validation Accuracy: 88.04%
      • Brier Calibration Loss: 0.0968
      • Centers: Cleveland Clinic, Hungarian Inst., Univ. Zurich, Long Beach VA, St. Carlos
      • Limitation: Observational cohort; correlational importances do not imply causation.

      📚 Clinical Guidelines Inventory

      • 2021 AHA/ACC Chest Pain Guideline: hs-cTn protocols, HEART score triage, CXR indications
      • 2022 AHA/ACC Heart Failure Guideline: 4-Pillar GDMT, LVEF staging, diagnostic workup
      • 2019 ATS/IDSA Pneumonia Guideline: CURB-65 triage, CXR signs, outpatient/inpatient therapy
      • Vector Database: FAISS Inner Product index with Sentence-Transformers embeddings
      • Traceability: Explicit section and page citations on every claim
